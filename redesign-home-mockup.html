Our Story

Interrupted by War. Called Back by Hope.

Since 2006, Y.E.T. Compassion has served the people of South Sudan — drilling wells, delivering food aid and medical supplies, and supporting schools in the Biong community.

When war broke out in 2013, families were displaced and the work on the ground had to stop. Today, conditions have opened the door to return — and we are resuming the mission where it matters most: educating the next generation.
